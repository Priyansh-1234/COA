The design must follow the hierarchical module structure specified above.
Each functionality must be implemented in its corresponding Verilog module, and larger
modules must instantiate the required lower-level modules.
Students may use suitable combinational Verilog constructs inside the individual modules,
including:
• gate primitives;
• continuous assignment using assign;

• case statements.
