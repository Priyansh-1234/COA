3. Required Adder Connections
The partial products must be combined as follows.
1. The least significant product bit is

2. Add p10 and p01 using a Half Adder:
(P1 , C1 ) = HA(p10 , p01 ).
3. Add p20 , p11 , and p02 using a Full Adder:
(S2 , C2a ) = F A(p20 , p11 , p02 ).
Then combine S2 and C1 using a Half Adder:
(P2 , C2b ) = HA(S2 , C1 ).
4. Add p21 , p12 , and C2a using a Full Adder:
(S3 , C3a ) = F A(p21 , p12 , C2a ).
Then combine S3 and C2b using a Half Adder:
(P3 , C3b ) = HA(S3 , C2b ).
5. Finally, add p22 , C3a , and C3b using a Full Adder:
(P4 , P5 ) = F A(p22 , C3a , C3b ).
Thus, the complete output is
P = P5 P4 P3 P2 P1 P0 .

Verilog Design Requirement
Both questions must follow the specified hierarchical module organization.
Students may use gate primitives or continuous Boolean assignments inside the Half Adder
and Full Adder modules. However:
• the Array Multiplier must generate and combine the individual partial products explicitly;
• the Verilog multiplication operator * must not be used to implement the multiplier;
• the Carry-Save Adder must use Full Adder instances for the carry-save reduction stage;
• the final addition in the Carry-Save Adder must use the specified Ripple-Carry Adder; and
• the complete arithmetic function must not be replaced by a single behavioral arithmetic
expression.
All designs must be purely combinational. No clocked or sequential logic is required.
Demonstration is mandatory. Each group must demonstrate both working designs and their
behavioral simulations to a Teaching Assistant.
Students may be asked to explain:
• partial-product generation in the Array Multiplier;
• how the Half Adders and Full Adders combine the partial products;
• why carries are not propagated between Full Adders in the Carry-Save stage;
• why the Carry vector must be shifted left by one position; and
• how the final Ripple-Carry Adder produces the final result.
